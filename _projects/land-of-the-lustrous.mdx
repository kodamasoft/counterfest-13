# Zn₂Fe(PO₄)₂·4H₂O -a Land of the Lustrous tribute album-

“Once you've crossed that bridge, burn it.” Zn₂Fe(PO₄)₂·4H₂O is a concept album based on the masterpiece by Haruko Ichikawa, “Land of the Lustrous”.  Within it, you will find image songs inspired by the original manga’s story, as well as arrangements of the animated series. This album, reflective of the beauty and purity of its inspiration, will record Phosphophyllite’s world and leave an eternal trace of beauty onto our own.

<WrappableParagraph title="Presentation">
“Zn₂Fe(PO₄)₂·4H₂O”, simply “Phosphopylite”, is a conceptual “image” album set in the world of Land of the Lustrous. Most songs are intended to be original and inspired by the story’s universe, characters, settings, and how it would “sound like” in your head. There is no limit to genres, and your intentions must be in the right place to create something which mirrors the world of Land of the Lustrous in an appropriate way. However, it will also function like any other tribute album of ours as well. You are free to remix, ressample and reuse elements from the animated series as you see fit.
</WrappableParagraph>

<WrappableParagraph title="Zn₂Fe(PO₄)₂·4H₂O Specific Music Rules">
Your submission must be only related to Land of the Lustrous. Please avoid cross-referencing different media sources which might overlap with the intention of the album being Land of the Lustrous.

For image/original tracks, this is kind of a blurry line, but the only requirement is that your track needs to “feel related” to the aimed product in some way. The general idea behind original/image work is that you need to compose and arrange your track in spirit with the Series, a character, a place, a scene, etc. For those kinds of submissions, you will need to add a comment to explain how your track is related to whatever product you aim to represent.

If you intend to reuse anything from the anime, please check the track claiming Google Sheet page to add the track you wish to reuse.

Your track needs to be at least 2 minutes long, and cannot exceed 14 minutes.

Due to the very specific nature of this album, we would be happy if people could provide as many songs as they wish to - even if they might be short, experimental

Kyoumen no Nami (the anime’s OP song) might already be planned to be remixed by a lot of people. It’s alright if the song is reused across the album, but we would like to avoid the album’s tracklist being filled with nothing but said song.
</WrappableParagraph>
